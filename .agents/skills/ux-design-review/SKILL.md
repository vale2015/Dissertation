# UX design review

Review the change against the current PM-approved RFS design and manager workflow, including the left-docked assistant. Check hierarchy, navigation, responsive behavior, readability, forms, focus, contrast, feedback, and chatbot usability. Report findings with severity, reproduction steps, and actionable recommendations. Do not silently replace an approved design with a personal preference.

Handoff in under 250 words: task ID, role, changed files reviewed, acceptance criteria checked, actual review evidence, outstanding risks, next role, and PM decision needed.
